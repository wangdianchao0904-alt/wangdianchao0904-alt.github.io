01 Overview scope & purpose

This is a private working notebook for my own research output: how it is being read, cited, contested, or extended in the field. It is not a publication record — the public site already lists papers and patents. Here I keep the analysis behind those numbers: who is citing what, in which context, and what that tells me about where the field is moving.

Sources I rely on: Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus, Dimensions, plus occasional manual sweeps of recent preprints. Numbers are refreshed roughly every two months and dated below.
